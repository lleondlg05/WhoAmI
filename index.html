HELLO WORLD!

My professional career as a software developer began in 2023, although my journey with programming started much earlier. In 2015, I began teaching myself to code, driven by my passion for computers and video games. It was during that time that I discovered what would eventually become my profession: solving problems through software development.

Along the way, I've had the privilege of being mentored by a senior developer with 15+ years in the industry, having worked at studios like 2K, Electronic Arts, Tuenti, and The Game Kitchen — that guidance shaped a lot of how I approach problems today.

Thanks to an opportunity and a vote of confidence, I landed my first role as a Unity tools developer. A year later, in 2024, I took the next step by specializing in UI/UX development for Brushbolt, where I've also kept building internal tools and porting code between C# and C++ — a language I continue to deepen my knowledge of. I'm interested in every branch of programming except web: tooling, porting, systems-level work, and game development are where I feel most at home.
